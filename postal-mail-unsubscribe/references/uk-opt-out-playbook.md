# UK postal marketing opt-out playbook

## Your legal rights (UK)

- **UK GDPR, Article 21(2):** you have an absolute right to object to processing of your personal data for direct marketing. Once you object, the organisation must stop. There is no "legitimate interest" balancing test for marketing.
- **Data Protection Act 2018:** domestic framework that sits alongside the UK GDPR.
- **PECR 2003 (Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations):** governs electronic marketing; cite alongside GDPR for completeness.
- **Mailing Preference Service (MPS):** free service to reduce addressed advertising mail (mpsonline.org.uk). Useful as a blanket measure across many senders.
- **Escalation:** if mail continues after a documented opt-out, it can be reported to the **Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)**, ico.org.uk.

## How to find the right contact

1. Search: `{company} opt out postal marketing email` and `{company} data protection officer email`.
2. Prefer, in order: official help/opt-out page -> DPO/privacy email -> general support email.
3. Verify the address on an official source (company domain, help centre, or privacy policy). Do not guess.
