# Opt-out email template

**To:** {verified opt-out / DPO email}
**Cc:** {secondary privacy email, if one exists}
**Subject:** Opt-out request: please stop all postal marketing ({business name})

```
Dear {company},

I am writing to request that you stop sending all postal marketing and promotional letters to me and to my business.

Please remove the following details from all of your direct marketing lists, including postal mailings:

Name: {addressed name}
Business: {business name}
Address: {postal address as printed on the letter}

This is a formal objection to the processing of my personal data for direct marketing purposes under Article 21(2) of the UK GDPR, and a request under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003. Please confirm in writing once my details have been suppressed and that no further marketing post will be sent.

If any of my data has been obtained from a third-party data broker, please also tell me the source so I can opt out there as well.

Thank you.

Kind regards,
{user's full name}
```

## Notes
- Keep it plain text, British English, no em dashes.
- If the letter prints a customer/reference number, add a line: `Reference: {number}`.
- If the company documents specific required fields (e.g. email address on the account), include them.
